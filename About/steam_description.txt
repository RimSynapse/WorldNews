[h1]RimSynapse - WorldNews[/h1]
[b][url=https://github.com/RimSynapse/WorldNews/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]
[b]A dynamic newspaper that reports on colony gossip, psychological breaks, and geopolitical shifts using Large Language Models.[/b]

Bring the Rim to life with a local tabloid! WorldNews acts as a dynamic journalist, generating newspaper pages based on the actual history, wars, romances, and breakdowns occurring in your game.

---

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Dynamic Tabloid Reporting:[/b] Read generated articles covering marriage events, theological schisms, and dramatic pawn breakdowns.
[*] [b]Global News Columns:[/b] Reports on distant faction territory shifts, wars, and noble ascensions.
[*] [b]Historical Grounding:[/b] Integrates with all other RimSynapse companion mods to print gossip and headlines about your pawns' psychological events.
[/list]

---

[h2]Roadmap[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]0.8 - Demographics, News and Narrative Voice:[/b] The newspaper starts reporting the world map - a change feed of borders, outposts and ideology shifts, quest outcomes that become news factions react to, planetary news-feed and settlement-status tools, on-demand lore retrieval, and a dependency on Regions and Territories.
[*] [b]0.9 - Factions and Living Societies:[/b] Ideological artefacts abroad - the article, the conversion, and the war it might start.
[*] [b]0.10 - Cross-Mod Capability Passes:[/b] Another pass over how the news draws on every module.
[/list]
These ship when they are ready - no dates.

[h2]Compatibility Matrix[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Royalty DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Generates newspapers tracking royal ascensions and empire news.
[*] [b]Ideology DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Generates theological debate columns and festival coverage.
[*] [b]Biotech DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Generates genetic breakthrough and mechanitor threat articles.
[*] [b]Anomaly DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Generates creepy tabloid stories about void sightings.
[*] [b]RimSynapse - Factions:[/b] Compatible and Optional. WorldNews will dynamically report on the procedurally generated histories, schisms, and wars of your custom LLM factions.
[*] [b]RimSynapse - Psychology:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Newspapers will gossip about your colonists' psychological breakdowns and dramatic therapy sessions.
[/list]
